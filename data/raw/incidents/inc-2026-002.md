# INC-2026-002: Order confirmation delayed by Kafka lag

## Summary and impact

A production degradation affected order-service 3.7.2. Customers experienced delayed or failed operations and support observed KAFKA-301. The incident was contained without data loss.

## Timeline

- 14:27 alert crossed the error-budget threshold.
- 14:32 on-call correlated KAFKA-301 with the deployment and dependency dashboards.
- 14:48 the team reproduced the failure in a production-like canary.
- 15:06 containment reduced customer impact.
- 15:41 the permanent corrective change completed verification.

## Detection and logs

```text
2026-01-12T14:32:11Z order-service ERROR KAFKA-301 request_id=req-7f21 dependency_state=degraded waiting=127
```

## Investigation and root cause

The team eliminated gateway, authentication, and tenant-specific hypotheses by tracing complete requests. The confirmed root cause was a schema-registry timeout triggered repeated consumer restarts rather than the earlier rollout rebalance pattern. Similar symptoms can have different causes; in particular, INC-2025-007 and INC-2026-002 both involve Kafka lag but require different corrective actions.

## Resolution and recovery

The responders pinned the cached schema and raised registry circuit-breaker tolerance. They replayed only idempotent work, compared business counters before and after recovery, and kept the incident open until fifteen minutes of stable service-level indicators.

## Contributing factors

The pre-production load profile underrepresented production concurrency. Alert context showed the symptom but not the recently changed configuration. The change review also lacked a dependency-capacity calculation.

## Preventive actions

The owning team added a version-aware configuration test, a deployment canary, and a dashboard joining service replicas to dependency capacity. Future releases must record the calculation in release evidence.

Error code KAFKA-301; service guide for order-service; corresponding customer support tickets and current release notes.
