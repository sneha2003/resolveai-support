# INC-2025-024: Payment authorization ambiguity during processor latency

## Summary and impact

A production degradation affected payment-service 4.2.1. Customers experienced delayed or failed operations and support observed PAY-307. The incident was contained without data loss.

## Timeline

- 14:27 alert crossed the error-budget threshold.
- 14:32 on-call correlated PAY-307 with the deployment and dependency dashboards.
- 14:48 the team reproduced the failure in a production-like canary.
- 15:06 containment reduced customer impact.
- 15:41 the permanent corrective change completed verification.

## Detection and logs

```text
2025-08-09T14:32:11Z payment-service ERROR PAY-307 request_id=req-7f21 dependency_state=degraded waiting=127
```

## Investigation and root cause

The team eliminated gateway, authentication, and tenant-specific hypotheses by tracing complete requests. The confirmed root cause was the provider response crossed the client deadline after authorization committed. Similar symptoms can have different causes; in particular, INC-2025-007 and INC-2026-002 both involve Kafka lag but require different corrective actions.

## Resolution and recovery

The responders reconciled by idempotency key and disabled unsafe automatic retry. They replayed only idempotent work, compared business counters before and after recovery, and kept the incident open until fifteen minutes of stable service-level indicators.

## Contributing factors

The pre-production load profile underrepresented production concurrency. Alert context showed the symptom but not the recently changed configuration. The change review also lacked a dependency-capacity calculation.

## Preventive actions

The owning team added a version-aware configuration test, a deployment canary, and a dashboard joining service replicas to dependency capacity. Future releases must record the calculation in release evidence.

## Related records

Error code PAY-307; service guide for payment-service; corresponding customer support tickets and current release notes.
