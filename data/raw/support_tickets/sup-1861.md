# SUP-1861: BlueYonder Market

## Customer report

Priority P2. The customer wrote: “checkout keeps timing out after latest update even though payment pods look green.” Their production environment used payment-service 4.2 on Kubernetes with the standard Nexora dependency profile.

## Troubleshooting conversation

Support first confirmed scope, start time, affected tenants, and the exact upgrade window. The engineer requested request IDs and configuration values rather than secrets or full payloads. Healthy pods did not rule out downstream saturation. The engineer searched exact identifier DB-104 and then broadened the query using the customer's informal description.

## Diagnosis

The evidence matched INC-2025-018: 4.2 applied a fixed pool maximum of 40 per pod; with PostgreSQL 16 and 12 replicas this exhausted the 300-session database budget. Metrics and a controlled canary excluded unrelated gateway and authentication failures.

## Resolution

Support guided the customer through the approved containment and the permanent fix: reduced maximumSize to 24 and minimumIdle to 6, then deployed 4.2.1. The customer verified representative operations, business counters, and fifteen minutes of normal error and latency levels.

## Related documentation

INC-2025-018; DB-104 in DOC-ERR-001; current payment-service troubleshooting guide and release notes.
