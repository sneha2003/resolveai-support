# SUP-2210: Lumen Direct

## Customer report

Priority P2. The customer wrote: “receipts wait behind marketing mail.” Their production environment used notification-service 2.2 on Kubernetes with the standard Nexora dependency profile.

## Troubleshooting conversation

Support first confirmed scope, start time, affected tenants, and the exact upgrade window. The engineer requested request IDs and configuration values rather than secrets or full payloads. Healthy pods did not rule out downstream saturation. The engineer searched exact identifier NOTIFY-429 and then broadened the query using the customer's informal description.

## Diagnosis

The evidence matched INC-2026-006: provider quotas were shared across transactional and campaign traffic. Metrics and a controlled canary excluded unrelated gateway and authentication failures.

## Resolution

Support guided the customer through the approved containment and the permanent fix: split quota pools and prioritized transactional topics. The customer verified representative operations, business counters, and fifteen minutes of normal error and latency levels.

## Related documentation

INC-2026-006; NOTIFY-429 in DOC-ERR-001; current notification-service troubleshooting guide and release notes.
