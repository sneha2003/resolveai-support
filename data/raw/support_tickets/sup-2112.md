# SUP-2112: Juno Office

## Customer report

Priority P2. The customer wrote: “nightly import got rate limited after gateway upgrade.” Their production environment used api-gateway 7.1 on Kubernetes with the standard Nexora dependency profile.

## Troubleshooting conversation

Support first confirmed scope, start time, affected tenants, and the exact upgrade window. The engineer requested request IDs and configuration values rather than secrets or full payloads. Healthy pods did not rule out downstream saturation. The engineer searched exact identifier GW-429 and then broadened the query using the customer's informal description.

## Diagnosis

The evidence matched INC-2025-037: client identity normalization dropped the tenant partition from rate-limit keys. Metrics and a controlled canary excluded unrelated gateway and authentication failures.

## Resolution

Support guided the customer through the approved containment and the permanent fix: restored tenant-aware keys and replayed rejected batches. The customer verified representative operations, business counters, and fifteen minutes of normal error and latency levels.

## Related documentation

INC-2025-037; GW-429 in DOC-ERR-001; current api-gateway troubleshooting guide and release notes.
