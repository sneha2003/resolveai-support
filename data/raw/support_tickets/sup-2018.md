# SUP-2018: Evergreen Shop

## Customer report

Priority P2. The customer wrote: “bank approved a payment but our UI says it timed out.” Their production environment used payment-service 4.2.1 on Kubernetes with the standard Nexora dependency profile.

## Troubleshooting conversation

Support first confirmed scope, start time, affected tenants, and the exact upgrade window. The engineer requested request IDs and configuration values rather than secrets or full payloads. Healthy pods did not rule out downstream saturation. The engineer searched exact identifier PAY-307 and then broadened the query using the customer's informal description.

## Diagnosis

The evidence matched INC-2025-024: the provider response crossed the client deadline after authorization committed. Metrics and a controlled canary excluded unrelated gateway and authentication failures.

## Resolution

Support guided the customer through the approved containment and the permanent fix: reconciled by idempotency key and disabled unsafe automatic retry. The customer verified representative operations, business counters, and fifteen minutes of normal error and latency levels.

## Related documentation

INC-2025-024; PAY-307 in DOC-ERR-001; current payment-service troubleshooting guide and release notes.
