# SUP-1933: Cobalt Home

## Customer report

Priority P2. The customer wrote: “users are randomly signed out during traffic bursts.” Their production environment used authentication-service 5.0 on Kubernetes with the standard Nexora dependency profile.

## Troubleshooting conversation

Support first confirmed scope, start time, affected tenants, and the exact upgrade window. The engineer requested request IDs and configuration values rather than secrets or full payloads. Healthy pods did not rule out downstream saturation. The engineer searched exact identifier CACHE-118 and then broadened the query using the customer's informal description.

## Diagnosis

The evidence matched INC-2025-003: a volatile-lru policy evicted refresh-token state during a campaign traffic spike. Metrics and a controlled canary excluded unrelated gateway and authentication failures.

## Resolution

Support guided the customer through the approved containment and the permanent fix: reserved memory and changed the token cache to noeviction. The customer verified representative operations, business counters, and fifteen minutes of normal error and latency levels.

## Related documentation

INC-2025-003; CACHE-118 in DOC-ERR-001; current authentication-service troubleshooting guide and release notes.
